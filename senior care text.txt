Comprehensive Non-Medical Home Care Services
Personalized Daily Living Support, Guided & Supervised by Registered Nurses
At 24x7 Home Care, we provide reliable, compassionate non-medical support tailored to your loved one’s lifestyle and daily routines. Under the direct supervision of a Registered Nurse, our care plans ensure maximum safety, dignity, and peace of mind for the whole family.

Service Categories
1. Personal Care & Hygiene Support
Respectful, hands-on assistance designed to preserve dignity and comfort in daily routines.

Assistance with Bathing & Grooming: Safe support with showers, baths, hair care, and skin care.
Dressing & Oral Care: Helping clients look and feel their best each morning and evening.
Toileting & Incontinence Care: Discreet, sensitive assistance focused on cleanliness and hygiene.
Mobility & Transfer Assistance: Safe assistance getting into and out of bed, chairs, or wheelchairs to prevent falls.
2. Companionship & Household Support
Fostering connection and managing day-to-day household operations to keep the home comfortable.

Friendly Companionship: Engaging in conversation, hobbies, reading, or memory activities.
Nutritious Meal Preparation: Preparing healthy meals according to diet preferences and dietary restrictions.
Light Housekeeping & Laundry: Sweeping, tidying up, dishwasher loading, linen changes, and laundry.
Errands & Groceries: Assistance with grocery shopping, prescription pickups, and light errands.
3. Routine Support & Wellness Monitoring
Keeping routines organized and keeping families informed.

Medication Reminders: Gentle reminders to ensure timely intake of prescribed routines (non-administration).
Routine Tracking & Care Log: Daily updates logged by caregivers and reviewed regularly by our RN team.
Appointment Escort: Accompaniment to medical appointments, social outings, or family gatherings.
4. Specialized Family & Respite Care
Family Caregiver Respite: Flexible hours to give full-time family caregivers necessary rest and relief.
Post-Hospitalization Support: Transitional non-medical care helping clients return safely home after hospital discharge.
24/7 & Overnight Care: Round-the-clock home coverage for clients needing continuous oversight and companionship.
